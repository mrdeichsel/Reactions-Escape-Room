Stockroom 204

The stockroom door sealed itself during inventory, and tomorrow’s lab needs what’s inside. The lock only opens for chemists who can label the shelf, fix the reaction log, and identify the unknown samples. Each room you clear fills one section of the hazard diamond. Fill all four to get out.

Before you go in

Hints cost one minute each. Wrong answers are free, but they’re counted. Rooms open one at a time.

For teachers

Every run draws a different set of questions from a larger bank, so neighboring teams won’t have identical puzzles. Room 1 has 6 of 14 formulas, Room 2 has 4 of 9 equations plus a redox challenge, Room 3 has 6 reactions (always including one that doesn’t happen), and the door uses 1 of 4 final reactions.

Progress saves on this device, so a refresh won’t lose it. “Start over” at the bottom of the page clears it. The finish screen shows time, hints, and wrong attempts for you to check.
